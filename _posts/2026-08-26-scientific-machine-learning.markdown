Over the past decade, the 19th century science-of-counting has been resurrected to provide a derivation for Machine Learning that generalizes statistics to probability theory, responds to energy that enters or exits the system (not mechanical/statistical), and can process any time-series to return a complete set of scientific measurements as deductive Reality.  

Conventional machine learning is impressively effective in static, closed world applications, but cannot accommodate environments where energy can enter or exit.  

Ten years ago, we purchased time-series for SML processing, for example, closing prices on equities, and then four hours after market close, SML processed every stock on the NYSE and NASD in five minutes, and provided a complete set of scientific measurements to the customer as a file, every trading day for over six years.
